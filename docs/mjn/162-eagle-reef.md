# Dive 162: Eagle Reef, Catalina, CA, USA
#### Date: 11 November 2026
#### Latitude: 33°27'42.9"N 
#### Longitude: 118°30'16.2"W
#### Dive Number: 162
#### Overview
##### Type: Ocean, Boat Dive (Makai Mist)
##### Partner: Amy R.
##### Participants: MJN, ALR, AJN, TGN

Another great dive on Eagle Reef. Topped my last statement at this dive site with again the clearest, deepest blue water, I have every seen on the island. Anchored in 85 feet and could see the bottom. Greater than 25 meter visibility. Incredibly warm water for this time of year becasue of a very stron El Nino. Anchored in just south of the red marker buoy. Descended anchor line then moved east, ultimately circling back to the anchor line to end the dive. Nice navigation. Thousands, thousands, of fish. Huge school of Blacksmiths throughout the dive. Literally surrounded by a huge schools cascading up and down as we navigated. Surprised a swimming Morray Eeel as I crested a rock outcrop. Tobias deftly handled a sticky low-pressure inflator. Very beautiful dive. Great reef structure and geology. Morray Eels, Pacific Octopus. Sea lions. Everyone too "sick" to return to the mainland, so we extended the trip for a day for more diving. Amazing, world class, dive.
