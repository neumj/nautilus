# Dive 163: Avalon Underwater Park, Catalina, CA, USA
#### Date: 12 November 2026
#### Latitude: 33°20'57.2"N 
#### Longitude: 118°19'30.5"W
#### Dive Number: 163
#### Overview
##### Type: Ocean, Boat Dive (Makai Mist)
##### Partner: Asher N.
##### Participants: MJN, ALR, AJN, TGN

Dive at the Avalon Underwater Park on our extended mini-dive holiday. Great visibility, very warm water under a strong El Nino condition. Moored in Descanco Mooring Field on W-1. East into the dive park to 23 meters then ascend to approximately 12 meters and return west to just under the boat. Little to no kelp, interesting to do this site without kelp. Many fish, some tropical species I have never seen. Asher deftly handled an equalization issue mid-dive. Sea lion. Beautiful, relaxing dive. On the retrun to the mainland, caught a 36 inch Dorado mid-channel. Great trip!
